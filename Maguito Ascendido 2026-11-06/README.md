## What's in here

- **`world/`** — everything you wrote, as Markdown. Open it in any editor
  (Obsidian, iA Writer, VS Code) or just read it here. The folders match
  your sidebar, and documents link to each other.
- **`media/`** — your original images, audio and video, exactly as uploaded.
- **`Maguito Ascendido.vvd`** — your world's data, in vvd's own format.
  It holds the parts Markdown can't carry: maps, canvases, timelines,
  relationships, types and settings. Keep it if you ever want this world
  back — it's the file vvd reads.

Nothing here needs vvd to open except the last one. Your writing is yours,
in a format that will outlive us.
